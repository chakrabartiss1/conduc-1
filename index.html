🔮 Peeling Back of Refractoriness

  • Mechanism: A premature impulse ($S_2$) penetrates proximal tissue (e.g., AV node / His bundle) and encounters refractoriness or conducts slowly.
  • Downstream Refractory Shift: Because $S_2$ alters the local repolarization timing, it repolarizes downstream tissue earlier, effectively "peeling back" its refractory boundary.
  • S3 Conduction Window: A subsequent premature impulse ($S_3$) can then conduct through downstream tissue earlier than it otherwise could!

🔍 The Gap Phenomenon Paradox

  • The Paradox: An extra-stimulus at a shorter interval ($S_2 = 270\text{ ms}$) conducts, whereas an impulse at a longer interval ($S_2 = 340\text{ ms}$) blocks!
  • 4-Tier Transition:
    • At $340\text{ ms}$: Fast AV conduction $\rightarrow$ Strikes distal bundle in Red (ARP) $\rightarrow$ Distal Block.
    • At $270\text{ ms}$: Incurs severe AV delay in Yellow (Proximal Brake) $\rightarrow$ Distal bundle recovers to Green/Yellow $\rightarrow$ Conducted with Wide QRS!

⚡ R-on-T Vulnerability & VF Engine

  • Vulnerable Window: 180--280 ms during repolarization (T-wave peak-to-end).
  • Re-entry Trigger: A PVC landing in this window triggers chaotic polymorphic VT / VF across both rhythm strips!
